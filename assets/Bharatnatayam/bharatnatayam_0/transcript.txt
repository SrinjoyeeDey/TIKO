Today I will be giving you a glimpse into the Indian classical art form called bharatanatyam.
So you might ask, what does bharatanatyam mean?
Bharatanatyam originated from the state of Tamil Nadu in India and is one of the oldest dance forms with a rich and colorful heritage.
Bharatanatyam and many other terms we will be using today is in Tamil, one of the oldest languages in the Indian subcontinent.
Now, the word bharatanatyam has four syllables.
Bha, ra, ta, natyam.
Bha or bhava means expression.
Imagine you have an ice cream and somebody snatches it from you.
And what is your reaction?
Now, that's an expression.
Ra or raga or ragam means music.
Ta or thala or thalam means beats.
And natya or natyam means dance.
So bharatanatyam is a beautiful combination of expression, music, beats, and dance coming together, creating this complete harmony.
